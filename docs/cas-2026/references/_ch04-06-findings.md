## Notable Findings

### Chapter 4 — Techniques

- **TCAR is now co-equal with TFCAS in current guidelines.** SVS 2022 CPG (PMID 34153348) and ESVS 2023 (PMID 35598721) codify TCAR alongside transfemoral CAS as an acceptable alternative to CEA for asymptomatic >=70% stenosis when perioperative stroke/death risk <3%. Any 2022/2024 slide presenting TFCAS as the only endovascular option is outdated; the decision tree is three-armed: CEA / TCAR / TFCAS.
- **Aortic arch classification content.** No standalone 2021–2026 high-impact review was found specifically on arch type I/II/III catheter strategy. The topic is embedded in ESVS 2023 and SVS implementation document (PMID 34153349), which covers guide catheter selection and access challenges based on arch morphology.
- **TCAR outcomes (Galyfos 2021, PMID 33674158; n=4,852).** Pooled 30-day stroke rate 1.4%; symptomatic patients had 2x higher cerebrovascular event risk vs. asymptomatic (OR 1.99, p=0.046). TCAR is not risk-free in symptomatic patients — important training point.
- **Best current single reference for Ch4: Dixit et al. 2026 (PMID 42073411).** Full-text XML available locally. Covers TCAR, stent design, EPD, all major trial evidence synthesis, and the impact of intensive medical therapy on revascularization thresholds.
- **Intensive medical therapy reframing.** 2024 ESC guideline (PMID 39210722) explicitly states that for many asymptomatic patients, optimized medical therapy may match or exceed revascularization benefit. This paradigm change is not captured in pre-2024 slides.

### Chapter 5 — EPD

- **Proximal protection is superior for DWI imaging but NOT clinical stroke.** Giannopoulos 2024 network meta-analysis (PMID 36394946; n=7,600, 45 studies): proximal balloon had significantly fewer new DWI ischemic lesions than distal filter (OR 3.15, p=0.002) or distal balloon (OR 2.28, p<0.001). No statistically significant difference in clinical TIA or stroke between any EPD types. The imaging-vs-clinical dissociation is the central teaching point for this chapter.
- **No EPD type has proven clinical superiority.** Fioretti et al. 2022 (PMID 36700668): after reviewing all EPD clinical trials, no convincing evidence supports one EPD over another for clinical outcomes. MoMa and flow-reversal devices have mechanistic advantages but practical limitations.
- **Layered protection paradigm — key 2023 conceptual update.** Micciche et al. 2023 (PMID 37000987): optimal CAS embolic protection requires EPD selection PLUS dual-layer stent. Distal filters protect only during the procedure; dual-layer stents protect post-EPD removal. This is a paradigm shift from single-device to combined protection strategy; not reflected in pre-2023 slides.
- **TCAR as continuous flow-reversal EPD.** Lewis et al. 2025 (PMID 39343373): ENROUTE system provides continuous proximal flow reversal throughout all procedural phases, eliminating unprotected intervals of conventional EPDs during stent deployment.
- **DWI lesion gap with CEA persists.** Loufopoulos et al. 2024 (PMID 38825067; 25 studies, 3,327 patients): CEA had significantly fewer new DWI ischemic lesions than CAS at all timepoints (OR 0.24–0.33, p<0.001). EPD use reduced but did not eliminate the gap.

### Chapter 6 — Stent Design

- **Dual-layer stents are the current best-evidence CAS platform.** Mazurek et al. 2022 (PMID 36013058; 112 studies, 68,422 patients): SGS vs. FGS 30-day DSM 1.30% vs. 4.11% (p<0.001); 30-day stroke 0.6% vs. 3.01% (p<0.001). Any lecture using CREST/SPACE-era stroke rates to characterize contemporary CAS risk is misleading.
- **No "mesh stent class effect" — critical teaching point.** CGuard MicroNet reduced both 30-day stroke AND 12-month ISR vs. FGS (both p<0.001). Casper/Roadsaver reduced 30-day stroke but significantly INCREASED 12-month ISR (+3.19%, p=0.04). Gore mesh stent was neutral for all endpoints. Dual-layer stents are NOT interchangeable.
- **Real-world validation.** Stefanini et al. 2024 (PMID 38727642; n=1,101 procedures, prospective Italian registry): CGuard stroke 1.18%, Roadsaver 2.63% vs. FGS 4.59% (p=0.008). Confirms meta-analytic findings in unselected real-world population.
- **Roadsaver restenosis signal requires slide update.** ISR >7% at 12 months (rising to 13% at 24 months in some series) after Casper/Roadsaver requires vigilant 6- and 12-month duplex ultrasound surveillance. Not reflected in pre-2022 slides.
- **2026 stent design review (Dubosq-Lebaz et al., PMID 41866683).** Most current stent taxonomy: open-cell designs preferred for tortuous anatomy; dual-layer designs sacrifice some conformability for enhanced plaque coverage. Drug-eluting carotid stents remain investigational with no guideline endorsement as of 2026.
